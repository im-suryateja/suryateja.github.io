Car price and mileage by state and region

Statewise and regional analysis of used-car asking prices and odometer readings, done in R.

  • R
  • ANOVA

Description

This project uses a real-world dataset of used cars listed across U.S. regions and states to analyze the factors that affect asking price and odometer reading. ANOVA and Tukey's HSD post-hoc tests compare means across states, fuel types, vehicle conditions and regions.

Methods

  • Levene's test for homogeneity of variances across regions.
  • One-way ANOVA.
  • Post-hoc Tukey HSD analysis.
