Effects of privatization on space missions

How the space industry changed over time as private companies entered the field.

  • Tableau
  • Data visualization

Approach

  • Analyzed the impact of privatization on space missions, focusing on private companies such as SpaceX, Blue Origin and Virgin Galactic.
  • Privatization brought innovation, cost efficiency and new commercial opportunities, which sped up space exploration.
  • Used datasets from the Center for Strategic and International Studies and NASA, with Tableau for the visualizations.

Key findings

  1. Mission failure rates have dropped since 2004.
  2. The cost per kilogram of launching to space has fallen.
  3. The number of space missions has grown, especially after 2016.
