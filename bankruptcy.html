Bankruptcy prediction with machine learning

A predictive model that assesses the risk score of a company.

  • Machine learning
  • Predictive analysis

The problem

  • Insurance companies need to earn customer trust in their risk assessment, which calls for transparent and reliable risk management.
  • Competition in the insurance market raises the bar for risk assessment methods.
  • Swings in the economy threaten the financial stability of insurance firms and call for stronger risk mitigation.

The approach

  • Data mining techniques such as Random Forest can predict bankruptcy risk accurately and help firms make informed decisions.
